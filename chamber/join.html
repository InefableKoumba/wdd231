NP Membership (Non-Profit)

Cost: Free (0 FCFA / $0)

  • Listing in the public online Chamber Directory
  • Access to monthly networking mixers
  • Quarterly non-profit sector roundtable discussions
  • Chamber badge for your website

Bronze Membership

Cost: 50,000 FCFA / year ($100/yr)

  • Standard Directory listing with website link
  • Invitation to general member workshops & seminars
  • 10% discount on Chamber event tickets
  • Member spotlight in monthly newsletter (1x per year)

Silver Membership

Cost: 150,000 FCFA / year ($300/yr)

  • Highlighted Directory listing with logo display
  • 20% discount on Chamber event tickets and booth rentals
  • Home page spotlight rotation (2x per year)
  • Access to VIP networking events with civic leaders
  • Sponsorship opportunities at Chamber luncheons

Gold Membership

Cost: 300,000 FCFA / year ($600/yr)

  • Premium top-tier Directory placement with custom bio & banner
  • Permanent Home Page Spotlight Position
  • Free access to all Chamber training programs for up to 5 staff
  • VIP reserved seating at annual business summit
  • Direct advisory board representation & policy input
